# FleetPilot Vision

FleetPilot is an AI-powered trucking operating system that connects fleet operations, dispatch, safety, accounting, maintenance, communications, and task execution in one platform.

## Product goal

Give a trucking company one reliable operating layer where every important event can become an owned, trackable task.

Examples include:

- late or rejected loads
- truck preventive maintenance
- tire and inspection issues
- driver document renewals
- insurance expirations
- unpaid invoices
- settlement reviews
- repair orders
- Amazon Relay performance issues
- email and Telegram follow-ups

## Operating principle

FleetPilot should turn operational signals into accountable work:

1. An event enters the system from a user, email, integration, schedule, or AI process.
2. FleetPilot determines whether action is required.
3. A task is created or updated.
4. The task is assigned to the correct person or team.
5. A due date, priority, and business context are attached.
6. Progress and decisions are recorded in an immutable activity timeline.
7. Escalations and notifications occur when work is late or blocked.

## Product values

- Operational truth over scattered messages
- Clear ownership over informal follow-up
- Auditability over hidden actions
- Automation with human control
- Multi-company support from the beginning
- Simple workflows that can grow into advanced workflows

## Long-term outcome

FleetPilot becomes the daily command center for the company. Owners and managers can see what requires attention, who owns it, what changed, what is late, and what the AI recommends next.
